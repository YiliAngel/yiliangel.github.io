Overview

A wearable soft exoskeleton that restores grasping to people with spinal cord injury (SCI), driven by the wearer's own decoded intent. The aim is to help SCI patients regain functional hand skills for the activities of daily living — and, longer term, to close the loop from neural intention to physical assistance.

What I'm Building

  • Soft pneumatic finger actuators (silicone-cast) and a layer-jamming wrist brace, for compliant, stable grasp assistance.
  • The embedded control and pressure-sensing stack that drives the wearable.
  • A neural-intent (BCI) pipeline that turns decoded intention into adaptive assistance.
  • A co-design study with clinicians, to ground the design in real rehabilitation needs.

An early-stage workshop paper on this work — “From Intention to Action” — was presented at the Everyday Wearables for Health workshop, CHI '26.
